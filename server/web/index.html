Chat rules

  • Be kind. Players of every age hang out here.
  • Keep it private. Never share your real name, address, phone number or passwords. The chat blocks e-mail addresses and phone numbers for you.
  • No links. Links are blocked so nobody gets tricked into a fake site.
  • See something bad? Tap the flag on the message. A moderator gets a ticket right away.

Before you report

  • Update the game. Many problems are already fixed in the newest version.
  • One problem per report. It helps us fix each one faster.
  • Add a screenshot if you can. Press PrtSc on Windows.
  • Save your ticket code. You need it to check on your report.
